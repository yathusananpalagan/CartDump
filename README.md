# CartDump

**CartDump** is a work-in-progress mod for **R.E.P.O** that adds a **Quick Deposit** action to the in-game **Cart**, letting you instantly empty it without dragging it all the way to the extraction point.

⚠️ **This mod is still in active development. Expect bugs and incomplete features.**

---

## 📦 What CartDump Does

- Adds a **Quick Deposit** action to the cart: instantly empties it (all items fall to the ground) and temporarily removes the cart so it doesn't block your way
- Only works while the cart is in **Strong mode** (actively being handled) — not in **Weak/Dragged mode**, to prevent accidental use while just pulling the cart around
- Fully **multiplayer-synced** — Quick Deposit plays out identically for everyone in the lobby, not just locally

---

## ⏱ Current Behavior

- Only usable while the cart is in **Strong mode**
- Walk up to the cart and press **F** while the prompt is visible
- The prompt shows briefly when conditions are met, then hides itself automatically after a few seconds
- The cart disappears, items inside fall to the ground, and the cart reappears near you after a short delay
- Timers and range are still being tuned — expect these to change

---

## 🛠 Planned Features
